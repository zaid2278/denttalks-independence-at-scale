What this series is about

Build a stronger practice before you decide what comes next.

Independent owners are being asked to solve more complex problems in technology, revenue cycle, staffing, compliance, cybersecurity, data and capital. DentTalks brings those subjects into one structured curriculum—and looks at the full spectrum between “go it alone” and “sell everything.”

Explore ownership, alliances, aggregation, M&A and liquidity without assuming one path is right for every owner.

Who should attend

Built for the full spectrum of independent ownership.

The same problem looks different at different scales. The curriculum deliberately addresses each level.

Single-office owners

Build minimum viable capability without recreating a corporate office.

2–5 location groups

Standardize before complexity compounds faster than management capacity.

5–20+ location groups

Institutionalize reporting, leadership, data, specialist functions and governance.

Worldwide viewers

Use the principles globally while recognizing that legal and transaction structures vary by jurisdiction.

Operators, not spectators

Presented from the intersection of dentistry, operations, technology and transactions.

Clark Caflisch

DSO operations, alliances, M&A and dental technology

Clark brings current and prior DSO operating leadership, Alliance formation and transaction experience, governance and integration expertise, and a long history in dental practice-management technology.

Dr. Vajahat “VJ” Yar Khan

Dentist-operator, scaled services and technology

VJ brings the perspective of a practicing dentist and multi-location operator, along with experience building scaled revenue-cycle/back-office services and dental technology infrastructure.

DentTalks

Education stays education.

DentTalks is the media and educational platform. Sessions are designed to deliver useful knowledge even for dentists who never use an affiliated service, join a platform or pursue a transaction.

Independence@Scale

Business exploration is separate.

Independence@Scale is a separate member-owned business initiative. Where accredited CE is offered, sales, investment discussions and business solicitation remain outside the CE session. Interested owners can request a separate, counsel-approved briefing.
